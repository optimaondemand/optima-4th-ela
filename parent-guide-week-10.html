This Week at a Glance

Anne hosts Diana for tea and serves her currant wine by mistake, and Mrs. Barry forbids the girls to see each other. Then, on a winter night, Anne saves Diana’s little sister from croup, and everything changes. She also wins over Diana’s grumpy great-aunt, and scares herself silly in a “Haunted Wood” she invented. This is also the week your student starts writing their story.

Reading skill
Comparing character perspectives. Your student looks at how Anne, Diana, Marilla, and Mrs. Barry see the same events differently.
Growing-up theme
Courage. Acting when others freeze, owning up to a mistake, and facing a fear (even one you made up).

“You forget that Mrs. Hammond had twins three times.”Anne Shirley, Chapter 18, before saving Minnie May

Good to know: In Chapter 16, Diana drinks three glasses of what she thinks is raspberry cordial and goes home drunk. The book plays it as an honest mix-up. Marilla had put the cordial in the cellar.

Words to Know

Use them at home!
incapablenot able; lacking the ability to do something
cordialwarmly friendly (also a sweet drink offered to friends)
relentto soften; to become less harsh; to change your mind
inexorablerefusing to give in or change; not moved by pleading
Word parts
il-, ir-, im-, in- all mean “not.”illegal · irregular · impossible · incorrect
Spelling
Hard and soft c and g. Before e, i, or y they go soft (/s/, /j/). Before a, o, or u they stay hard.cordial · cellar · golden · genius
Grammar
Present-tense verbs. I walk, she walks, they are walking. He, she, and it take the -s.Anne runs · they run

By Heart: This Unit’s Poem

Who has seen the wind?
Neither I nor you:
But when the leaves hang trembling,
The wind is passing through.Christina Rossetti

This is the poem your student is memorizing across the Anne unit. It comes up on Days 2 and 4, and each time a few more words fade from the screen.

How to help: Ask your student to say it to you. By now many students can say most of it. If they joined this week, that’s fine. The poem starts from the full text for every new student.

Ask at Home

Better than “How was school?”
  1. Was the currant wine Anne’s fault? Why did Mrs. Barry think so?Listen for: it was an honest mistake (Marilla had moved the cordial), but Mrs. Barry believed Anne did it on purpose.
  2. How did Anne know what to do when Minnie May was so sick?Listen for: she had cared for Mrs. Hammond’s three sets of twins, who all had croup.
  3. Anne and Diana both jumped on Miss Barry. Why did Anne go apologize by herself?Listen for: Anne said it was her idea. Compare it with her made-up confession in Week 9.
  4. Can you read me the first sentence of your story?Listen for: a first line that makes you want to hear the next one.

Try at Home

5 minutes
  • Sports announcer. Describe what someone is doing right now, in present tense: “Mom stirs the soup. The dog is barking.”
  • Hard or soft? Find c and g words on a cereal box or sign. Does the next letter make them hard or soft?
  • Not words. Add il-, ir-, im-, or in- to make opposites: legal, regular, possible, correct.
