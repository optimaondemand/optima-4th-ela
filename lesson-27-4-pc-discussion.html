✏️ Grammar: Conjunctions But & So
Explain how they work differently

You’ve been using conjunctions all week. Now let’s make sure you really understand the difference between but and so.

📌 The Difference

But = contrast. The second idea surprises or opposes the first.

So = cause and effect. The second idea happens because of the first.

📖 Examples from Prince Caspian
Miraz was king, but he had stolen the throne.
Caspian learned the truth about his uncle, so he fled the castle.
💬

“Teach It” Card: Imagine a younger student asks you: “What’s the difference between but and so?” How would you explain it in your own words? Think about it — no writing needed.

💭 No textbox here — just think through your explanation. You’ll use conjunctions in your writing assignment.
4
🔠 Spelling: Suffix Rules
Explain a rule in your own words

This week you’ve practiced suffix rules with multisyllabic words. Let’s review the big three rules.

📌 Three Suffix Rules

1. Drop the silent e: explore + -ing = exploring (not exploreing)

2. Double the final consonant: begin + -ing = beginning (stress on last syllable)

3. Change y to i: carry + -ed = carried (not carryed)

🧠

Explain It: Pick ONE suffix rule. In your own words, explain how it works and give an example. Think about it — no writing needed here.

💭 No textbox here — just make sure you understand the rule well enough to explain it.
