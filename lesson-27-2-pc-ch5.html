🌱 Morphology: Word Bases — Identify
Match words with their root meanings

A word base (or root) is the core part of a word that carries its main meaning. Knowing the base helps you decode unfamiliar words.

📌 Bases to Know

port = carry  |  tract = pull / draw  |  struct = build

Examples: transport (carry across), attract (pull toward), construct (build together)

✏️ Grammar: Conjunctions — but / so
Tap the conjunction in each sentence.

A conjunction joins two words, phrases, or ideas. But shows contrast. So shows cause and effect. Tap the conjunction in each sentence below.

Caspian wanted to stay at the castle, but Doctor Cornelius warned him that his life was in danger.
The Queen had a baby son, so Caspian’s life was in danger.
Cornelius gave him supplies, but the most important gift was the horn.
Caspian rode through the storm, but his horse stumbled in the dark.
Trufflehunter trusted Caspian, but Nikabrik did not.
Caspian was a Telmarine, so some Old Narnians didn’t trust him.

✍️ RWM — Read Like a Reader, Read Like a Writer, Mimic
“Dear Prince, you must leave this castle at once and go to seek your fortune in the wide world. Your life is in danger here.”
📖 Read Like a Reader
What is happening? Doctor Cornelius wakes Caspian in the middle of the night with this urgent message. Why is this moment so urgent? What has changed?
✏️ Read Like a Writer
Notice how Cornelius starts with “Dear Prince” — formal and tender. Then the rest is blunt and direct: “you must leave” and “your life is in danger.” What effect does this contrast between gentle address and hard truth create?
