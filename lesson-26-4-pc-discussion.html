✅ Correct Model — from Prince Caspian

Tap the highlighted parts to see what’s correct about them.
🔧 Now fix this one
the dwarf settled down and told his tale and the children listened carefully
Use the model above to help you. Rewrite this sentence correctly in your notebook.

Corrected: The Dwarf settled down and told his tale, and the children listened carefully.

Capital T on “The” (start of sentence). The first “and” doesn’t need a comma because it connects two verbs with the same subject (“settled down and told”). The second “and” DOES need a comma before it because it joins two complete sentences — “The Dwarf settled down and told his tale” and “the children listened carefully” each have their own subject and verb. Period at the end.

✏️ Grammar: Why Conjunctions Matter
Explain the importance

What happens when a writer doesn’t use conjunctions? Read these two versions of the same paragraph and notice the difference.

❌ Without Conjunctions
Peter drew his sword. Susan grabbed her bow. Edmund found his torch. Lucy held her cordial. They were ready.
✅ With Conjunctions
Peter drew his sword, and Susan grabbed her bow. Edmund found his torch, and Lucy held her cordial. Together, they were ready.
🧠 Think about it

What’s different? How do conjunctions change the way this paragraph sounds and feels? Without them, each sentence stands alone — choppy and separate. With them, the ideas flow together and the characters feel connected.

📋 Week at a Glance
What happened in each chapter
📖 Chapter 1: The Island

The four Pevensie children are pulled from a train station into a mysterious island. They find fresh water, wild apple trees, and ancient stone ruins half-buried in the forest.

📖 Chapter 2: The Ancient Treasure House

The children realize the ruins are Cair Paravel, their own castle from when they were Kings and Queens of Narnia. They find the treasure chamber and recover their gifts: Peter’s sword Rhindon, Susan’s bow and horn, and Lucy’s healing cordial.

📖 Chapter 3: The Dwarf

The children rescue a Dwarf named Trumpkin, who was about to be drowned by two soldiers. Trumpkin is on a mission and has a story to tell them about Prince Caspian and a rebellion.
