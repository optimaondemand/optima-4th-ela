📚 What Good Readers Do
Reflect on how reading habits have grown

You cannot see growth from one spot. You need two — a then and a now — and the gap between them is the growth.

So today you go back to the beginning of this year on purpose. Think about the first novel you opened and how it felt to be handed a whole book. Then think about the last one you finished. Being honest about the then is what makes the now mean something.

💡

How to Do It:

  1. Remember your September self: Write down what opening a whole novel felt like at the start of this year. Be honest, even if the honest answer is that it looked long and hard.
  2. Ask the same question about today: Now answer it again about the last book you finished. Same question, so the two answers can be compared.
  3. Join them with used to: Put both answers in one sentence: I used to … and now I … That sentence is your growth, in your own words.
🧠 As you work today, ask yourself:

Think about the first novel you opened this year and the last one you finished. Then finish this sentence honestly: I used to … and now I …

💭 Hold these questions in mind as you reflect on your growth on the Reading tab.

📌 What These Words Mean — Answer Key for All Five Books

pneumonia (Frisby) — a serious sickness in the lungs that makes breathing hard. Mr. Ages said that Timothy had pneumonia.
warily (Frisby) — in a careful way, watching out for danger. Mrs. Frisby climbed up the tunnel and looked around warily.
indignant (Anne) — feeling angry or annoyed because something is unfair. Anne felt indignant when Mrs. Lynde criticized her hair.
vexed (Anne) — troubled or irritated. Marilla was vexed when Anne forgot her chores.
solemnly (NTS) — in a serious, formal, or deeply sincere way. Annemarie solemnly promised to be brave.
occupation (NTS) — the control of a country by a military force of another nation. During the German occupation, the Jews of Denmark were in danger.
plantation (Freedom Train) — a large farm where one crop is grown, in the South often using slave labor. Harriet was born on the Brodas plantation in Maryland.
emancipation (Freedom Train) — the act of being set free. Harriet’s escape was her own emancipation.
allegiance (PC) — loyalty or commitment to a leader, country, or cause. The Old Narnians swore allegiance to Prince Caspian.
sovereign (PC) — a supreme ruler; having complete power. Aslan was the sovereign over all of Narnia.

✍️ RWM — Read Like a Reader, Read Like a Writer, Mimic
“Anne came dancing home in the purple winter twilight across the snowy places.”
📖 Read Like a Reader
Read the sentence out loud. What tone does it have — what mood does the writer seem to be in? Which single word tells you the most about that mood?
✏️ Read Like a Writer
Tone comes from word choice. Montgomery could have written “walked,” and she wrote dancing. She could have written “evening,” and she wrote purple winter twilight. She could have written “fields,” and she wrote snowy places. Every choice tips the sentence toward warmth and delight — that is the tone. Say it with plain words instead (“Anne walked home at dusk”) and the mood disappears, even though the facts stay the same. What tone would trudged have given it?
🖊️ Mimic
Write the sentence twice with the same facts and two different tones — once happy, once heavy. Change only the words, not the events.
[Character] came [action word] home in the [adjective] [time of day] across the [adjective] [place].
