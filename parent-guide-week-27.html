This Week at a Glance

The Dwarf the children rescued tells them a story, and for three chapters we hear it with them. Prince Caspian grows up in his uncle Miraz’s castle, where the old stories of Talking Beasts are forbidden. His tutor, Doctor Cornelius, tells him in secret that they are true. When Miraz’s own son is born, Caspian has to run for his life, and he finds the Old Narnians who have been hiding for hundreds of years. Each day your student also writes a short Writing Connection.

💛 A heads-up for families
In Chapter 5, Doctor Cornelius tells Caspian that Miraz murdered his father, and that Miraz will now want Caspian out of the way too. Caspian escapes at night. Later, a Dwarf named Nikabrik reaches for his dagger, and two friends stop him. Nobody is hurt.
Reading skill
Keeping track of groups of characters. Your student sorts every new character onto two lists, with Caspian or against him, and asks what each group wants.
Big idea
The truth kept alive in secret. A nurse, a tutor, a Badger and a Centaur have all held on to the old stories when it was dangerous to.

“Certainly it is true … Their reign was the Golden Age in Narnia and the land has never forgotten them.”Doctor Cornelius, Chapter 4

Words to Know

Use them at home!
tutora private teacher who teaches one student or a few
usurpersomeone who takes a throne without the right to it
astronomythe study of the stars, planets and sky
sternlyin a strict, serious way
Word parts
Latin bases: port (carry), tract (pull), struct (build).porter · tractor · structure
Spelling
Suffix rules. Drop the silent e, change y to i, and double the last consonant when the last syllable has the beat.hoping · emptier · beginning · visiting
Grammar
But and so. But brings a surprise; so tells what happened because of it. A comma goes before each when it joins two whole sentences.Caspian’s heart was sinking, but he tried to take it all in.

Writing This Week

Writing Connection

This week builds toward a comparison paragraph. Day 1 compares Miraz and Doctor Cornelius in two or three sentences; Day 3 practices moving a reader from one scene to the next, the way Lewis does; Day 4 is a full paragraph (five to seven sentences) comparing two characters, with but and so and a detail from the book.

Ask at Home

Better than “How was school?”
  1. Who is telling the story in these chapters, and who is listening?Listen for: the Dwarf (Trumpkin) is telling it to Peter, Susan, Edmund and Lucy.
  2. Why did Miraz send Caspian’s nurse away?Listen for: she told Caspian stories about Old Narnia, which Miraz had forbidden.
  3. Why did Caspian have to run away?Listen for: Miraz’s wife had a son, so Miraz wanted his own son to be King and Caspian out of the way.
  4. What did Doctor Cornelius give Caspian, and what did he say about it?Listen for: Queen Susan’s horn, to use only at his greatest need (and a purse of gold).
  5. Which Old Narnian did you like best, and why?Listen for: a named character (Trufflehunter, Reepicheep, Glenstorm…) and a reason from the book.
  6. Which pillar did you see most this week?Listen for: a pillar and a real moment from Chapters 4–6.
