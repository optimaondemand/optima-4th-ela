Weekly Fluency
The Old Narnians Gather

This week Caspian traveled across Narnia meeting the creatures who had been living in hiding — Badgers, Dwarfs, Centaurs, and Fauns. In this passage, the great Centaur Glenstorm speaks about the stars, and Caspian sees Fauns dancing in the moonlight for the first time. Read with the weight and wonder of creatures who have waited generations for this moment.

🎯
Objective
By the end of this lesson…
🎙️Fluency: I will read this passage accurately, smoothly, and with expression.
📖Standard: ELA.4.F.1.4 — Read grade-level texts with accuracy, automaticity, and prosody.
You’ll need: 🎙️ A device that records 📓 Reading Journal
1
📖 This Week’s Passage
From Chapter 6 of Prince Caspian — The People That Lived In Hiding
The great Centaur Glenstorm stood tall under the stars, his sons beside him. “The time is ripe,” he said. “I watch the skies, for it is mine to watch, as it is yours to remember. Tarva and Alambil have met in the halls of high heaven, and on earth a son of Adam has once more arisen to rule and name the creatures. The hour has struck.”

Later, when the night had grown deeper, they came to a place where the trees thinned and moonlight fell silver on the grass. And there Caspian saw what he had longed all his life to see — a Faun dance. The creatures moved lightly between the tree trunks, their little horns gleaming, their hooves barely touching the ground. It was like nothing he had ever imagined. It was Old Narnia, alive and real and right in front of him.

This passage is adapted from Chapter 6 of Prince Caspian by C. S. Lewis. Glenstorm speaks with the gravity of someone who has read the future in the stars. Then the mood shifts — the Fauns’ dance is light, magical, and full of wonder. Let your voice carry both: the weight of prophecy and the joy of something long hoped for finally coming true. — Ch. 6, Week 27

2
🎯 The 4 Steps
Work through these in order.
1️⃣

Read for accuracy. Read the passage out loud. Say every word correctly. Slow down where you need to — especially on “Tarva and Alambil” and “Glenstorm.”

1️⃣

Read for smoothness. Read it again. This time, try not to pause or stumble. Let Glenstorm’s speech flow in one steady stream.

2️⃣

Read with expression. Read it a third time. Let your voice shift between Glenstorm’s deep certainty and the lighter wonder of the Faun dance. Drop your voice for “The hour has struck” and let it lift for the moonlit scene.

3️⃣

Record and submit. Use Canvas’s recording tool to record your final reading. Upload it to this assignment.

3
🤔 Self-Check
Before you submit your recording.
Ask yourself:

Did I say each word correctly?

Did my reading sound smooth?

Did my voice show the feeling?

🎙️

If you can answer “yes” to all three, you’re ready to submit. If not, read it once more.
