🔧 Now fix this one
the centaurs were wise so the other creatures respected them but the bears were lovable for different reasons
Use the model above to help you. Rewrite this sentence correctly in your notebook.

Corrected: The centaurs were wise, so the other creatures respected them, but the bears were lovable for different reasons.

Capital T on “The” (start of sentence). Comma before “so” (cause-and-effect conjunction). Comma before “but” (contrast conjunction joining two complete thoughts). Period at the end. Both conjunctions in one sentence!
