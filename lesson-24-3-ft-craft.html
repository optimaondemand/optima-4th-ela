🌱 Morphology: Review Roots
pen/pens · fid · leg/lect — to weigh out, to trust, to gather/choose

These three roots come up everywhere in Harriet’s story and in history writing. pen/pens means to weigh out or pay; fid means to trust; leg/lect means to choose or gather.

📌 Quick Examples

pension (pen) = a payment weighed out over time  |  confident (fid) = trusting in oneself  |  elect (leg/lect) = to choose by vote  |  legend (leg) = a story gathered and passed down

✏️ Grammar: Prepositional Phrases
Day 3 — Write your own sentences using prepositional phrases.

A prepositional phrase starts with a preposition and ends with a noun or pronoun. It tells where, when, how, or for whom an action happens.

Harriet worked for the freedmen’s schools after the war.
Two prepositional phrases in one sentence! “for the freedmen’s schools” (preposition: for) tells for whom. “after the war” (preposition: after) tells when.
🎯

Tap the word that begins the prepositional phrase in each sentence. Prepositions include: after, before, beside, for, in, into, of, on, through, to, under, with.

Harriet sat beside her aging parents.
Nelson arrived in Auburn to work alongside Harriet.
She petitioned for her back pay again and again.

🔠 Spelling: Suffix & Syllable Review
Day 3 — Show What You Know (today’s graded response)

You’ve been studying suffixes and syllable patterns all week. These word endings are everywhere in Harriet’s story: pension, movement, courageous, fearless, government.

Suffix Examples from Ch. 19

-tion / -sion: pension, petition, emancipation
-ment: movement, government, settlement
-ous: courageous, famous, prosperous

What the Suffix Does

-tion / -sion makes a verb into a noun
-ment makes a verb into a noun
-ous makes a noun into an adjective

✏️ Show What You Know — Spelling
Choose three words from the list below. For each word: (1) write the base word, (2) name the suffix, (3) explain how the suffix changed the word’s meaning or part of speech.

Word choices: petition, movement, courageous, fearless, government, settlement, famous
Write three word analyses. Example: courageous — base: courage, suffix: -ous, change: turns the noun into an adjective meaning “full of courage.”
