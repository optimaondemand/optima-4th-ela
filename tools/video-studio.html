How to record a video

1. Set up (once)

  1. Open this page in Chrome and press Start camera. Choose Allow when Chrome asks.
  2. Pick your camera and microphone at the bottom right if the wrong ones show up.
  3. Face a window or lamp, with a plain wall behind you if you can.

2. Choose what you’re making

  • G4 shot list: pick a video on the left. It’s already named and knows where it goes.
  • Free record: type a name for any video. It saves to your Downloads folder.
  • Just me or Screen + me: Screen + me shows a tab or window with you in a corner.

3. Record

  • Press ● Record or Space. A 3-second countdown runs first.
  • P pauses and resumes. It all stays one video.
  • ✏ Draw (or D) lets you circle or underline things. Ink fades on its own unless you untick Fade.
  • Press ■ Stop or Space when you’re done.

4. Check it

  • Trim: drag the handles to cut the start and end.
  • Captions: fix any wrong words. Click a time to jump there.
  • Pick a thumbnail, then Keep it or Download now.

Your privacy

Your video never leaves this computer. It stays in this tab until you download or save it. The background tool downloads a small model from Google the first time, but nothing is sent back. The one exception is Make captions while I record: Chrome sends your audio to Google to turn speech into words. Leave it off and use Build from my script instead.

If something goes wrong

  • Choppy video or a slow computer? The studio switches to the Faster background on its own. Turning the background to None helps most.
  • No camera? Click the camera icon at the right end of Chrome’s address bar and choose Allow.
  • Closed the tab by mistake? Takes you didn’t download are gone, so download as you go.
