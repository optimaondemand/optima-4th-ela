This Week at a Glance

Anne goes to Sunday school in a plain dress with no puffed sleeves, and she meets Diana Barry. The two girls swear to be best friends forever. Anne is then blamed for losing Marilla’s amethyst brooch and makes up a false confession to get to a picnic. Finally she starts school, where Gilbert Blythe calls her “Carrots” and she cracks her slate over his head.

Reading skill
Cause and effect in character choices. Your student tracks how one choice (like trying on the brooch) leads to a bigger problem.
Growing-up theme
Friendship. Also: letting yourself look forward to things, and finding out what you would trade to get what you want.

“I solemnly swear to be faithful to my bosom friend, Diana Barry, as long as the sun and moon shall endure.”Anne Shirley, Chapter 12

Words to Know

Use them at home!
enrapturedfilled with great joy or delight; carried away by happiness
solemnserious; deeply earnest; not joking
resolutefirm and determined; not easily changed
vindictivewanting to get back at someone; full of revenge
Word parts
over- means “above” or “too much.” Review: sub- and under- mean “below.”overlook · overcoat · submarine · underground
Spelling
The schwa sound. The lazy “uh” in an unstressed syllable can be spelled by any vowel, so you can’t spell it by ear.about · lesson · garden
Grammar
Concrete and abstract nouns. Concrete nouns you can see or touch; abstract nouns name feelings and ideas.hat · despair · imagination

Ask at Home

Better than “How was school?”
  1. Why did Anne care so much about puffed sleeves?Listen for: every other girl had them, and Anne wanted to fit in instead of standing out.
  2. What did Anne and Diana do to make their friendship official?Listen for: they held hands in the garden and made a solemn vow to be friends forever.
  3. Anne confessed to something she didn’t do. Why, and how did that work out?Listen for: she wanted to go to the picnic. Marilla still said no, until the brooch turned up on Marilla’s own shawl.
  4. Gilbert called Anne “Carrots.” Why did that hurt so much?Listen for: Anne has always been teased about her red hair, like when Mrs. Rachel called it “red as carrots” in Week 8.

Try at Home

5 minutes
  • Touch it or feel it? Take turns naming nouns. Is it concrete (a spoon) or abstract (hope)?
  • Listen for “uh.” Say banana, lemon, pencil slowly. Which syllable goes lazy, and which letter spells it?
  • Cause and effect. Talk about a time one small choice led to a bigger result, good or bad.
