Study the model, then fix the sentence.

The first sentence is correct. Tap the parts you notice are done right — capital letters, punctuation, word choices. Then use it as a model to fix the second sentence.

✅ Correct Model — from Prince Caspian, Ch. 3
Susan’s arrow had struck on his helmet, and the soldier threw up his arms and fell over into the water.
Tap the highlighted parts to see what’s correct about them.
🔧 Now fix this one
susans arrow had struck on his helmet and the soldier throwed up his arms and fell over into the water
Use the model above to help you. Rewrite this sentence correctly in your notebook.

Corrected: Susan’s arrow had struck on his helmet, and the soldier threw up his arms and fell over into the water.

Capital S on “Susan’s” (proper noun + possessive apostrophe). Comma before “and” when it joins two complete thoughts. “Throwed” is not a word — the irregular past tense is “threw.” Period at the end. Two conjunctions in one sentence!

Passage from Chapter 3
“Like most Dwarfs he was very stocky and deep-chested. He would have been about three feet high if he had been standing up, and an immense beard and whiskers of coarse red hair left little of his face to be seen except a beak-like nose and twinkling black eyes.”
📖 Read Like a Reader

What does Trumpkin look like? What kind of person does he seem to be?

✏️ Read Like a Writer

Lewis packs a LOT of physical detail into two sentences. Notice how he organizes it: height first, then face. What effect does “beak-like nose and twinkling black eyes” create?

📖 Chapter Thinking
Answer in complete sentences with details from Chapter 3.
1Edmund figures out why the ruins look so ancient even though the children were only gone for one year. Explain his reasoning in your own words.
Think about how time works differently in Narnia vs. England.
2Susan shoots a soldier to save the Dwarf. This is a serious moment — she could have hurt someone badly. Why does Lewis include this scene? What does it show about Susan and about the kind of world Narnia has become?
Think about what the soldiers were about to do and what Susan’s action tells us.
3At the end of the chapter, Trumpkin introduces himself as a messenger of “King Caspian.” Based on everything you’ve learned so far, what do you think has happened in Narnia since the children left? Make a prediction using at least one detail from the chapter.
Think about the soldiers, the Dwarf, and the mention of a rebellion.
