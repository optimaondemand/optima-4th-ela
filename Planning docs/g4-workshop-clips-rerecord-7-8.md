## 7.2 — `matthew\g4ela-7-2-workshop.mp3` — Matthew Cuthbert

*Card: Writer’s Workshop: Week 7 · Day 2: Name, want, fear*

> Well now. A name, a want, and a fear. I never got the name out of that child, she had too much else to say. The want I had before we passed the pond: Green Gables. The fear she didn’t say. I reckon it was being sent back. I didn’t say mine either, and mine was the same one from the other side. Give your kid all three, and make the fear a real one.

## 7.3 — `marilla\g4ela-7-3-workshop.mp3` — Marilla Cuthbert

*Card: Writer’s Workshop: Week 7 · Day 3: Voice is word choice*

> Cordelia, if you please. And then Anne, but only with an e, as if one letter made a particle of difference. I never heard such talk in my life. But I’ll say this for the child: you’d not mistake her for anybody else on the Island. That’s what you’re after. Not nice. Not bad. Not went. The word your kid would pick, and nobody else. If it sounds like everybody, it’s wrong.

## 7.4 — `anne\g4ela-7-4-workshop.mp3` — Anne Shirley

*Card: Writer’s Workshop: Week 7 · Day 4: Voice under pressure*

> Disappointment! I’m quite an expert. Marilla was driving me away from the one place I ever wanted, and did I say I was sad? I did not. I said I’d made up my mind to enjoy the drive. I talked about the shore, which is what a person does when she can’t bear the other thing. What I said, what I did. Give your kid those, and what their body does too.

## 8.1 — `marilla\g4ela-8-1-workshop.mp3` — Marilla Cuthbert

*Card: Writer’s Workshop: Week 8 · Day 1: Show, don’t tell*

> Scared. Nervous. Those words tell me nothing, and I’d not have them in a story of mine. I’ll tell you what did tell me something. That child sat on Mrs. Spencer’s ottoman and never moved. Her face went white. And she didn’t say one word, which from her is as good as a shout. Nobody said frightened. I knew. Yours may talk. Take your feeling word out, and put in what a person would see.

## 8.3 — `rachel\g4ela-8-3-workshop.mp3` — Mrs. Rachel Lynde

*Card: Writer’s Workshop: Week 8 · Day 3: Where they come from, and who else is there*

> Two things. First, where your kid comes from. I called that child’s hair carrots and got a tempest for it, and Marilla said after that nobody had ever taught her better. Well, knowing where she came from, I believed it, that’s what. Two sentences of before, for your kid. Second, the other person. Every story needs one. Pick yours and say what they are to your kid. This chapter, it’s me.
