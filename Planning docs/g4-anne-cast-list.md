# Anne of Green Gables: guide cast list (draft)

**What this is:** who guides each of the 32 Anne lessons (weeks 7–14, Ch. 1–38) in character, and why. It's a draft for you to change; nothing is built or recorded from it yet.

## How each guide was chosen

1. **They were there.** A guide has to be in the chapter, or right beside what happens, so every line they say can be checked against the book.
2. **They watch Anne more often than they are Anne.** Most Big Questions ask students to work out what Anne feels from what she says and does. If Anne narrates, she would just tell them. So she guides only the lessons about her looking at herself.
3. **They know only the story so far.** A guide speaks from inside that chapter and can't hint at later ones. Matthew in week 7 doesn't know about the puffed sleeves.
4. **A small cast.** Every voice has to stay the same across the whole unit, so fewer voices means less to keep straight. This draft uses 9, and three of them appear only once or twice.

## Decisions for you

1. **Week 7.** Its clips are already recorded in the usual guide voice.
   - **Option A:** re-record week 7 in character as cast above. Matthew's 7.2 is nearly done, and the orientation video can say "this week".
   - **Option B:** start characters in week 8, and change one line of the video to "starting next week."
2. **14.3, the chapter where Matthew dies.** Marilla fits the Big Question about grief, but hearing it in a character's voice may land hard for some students. For that one lesson, the usual guide voice is the gentler choice. The text says what happens either way.
3. **Mrs. Allan (11.1).** Keep her for a single lesson, or give it to Marilla and have one voice fewer.
4. **Word study.** All 14 clips per lesson are in character, as you asked. If a character starts to feel forced on DOL or spelling, you can switch just those clips back to the usual guide voice, lesson by lesson.

## What I'd change in the layer before week 8

With 32 lessons in character, one small addition to `sparkle.js` saves setting up 14 audio entries by hand in each lesson's data file:
- **One line per lesson.** A single setting (for example `"guide": "marilla"`) picks the folder and puts "Marilla ·" on every button.
- **The name card and greeting match.** The name card asks in that character's voice, where today it says "what should Anne call you?".

It's the same kind of change as Read along, and I'd show you before making it.

**Checked against the novel:** every "why them" above is a scene in the chapter listed. I'll check each character's lines against the book when I write their scripts, the same way I check quotes.
