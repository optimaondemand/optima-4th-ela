# Week 7 orientation video: "Welcome to Avonlea"

**What it's for:** a short video students watch before lesson 7.1. It explains what's new on their lesson pages, especially the guides from the book.

**Length:** about 2½ minutes, roughly 350 spoken words.
**Voice:** the usual guide voice, not a character, so students hear who's "outside" the story before they meet the people inside it.
**Visuals:** screen recording of lesson 7.2 (it has the most features switched on), with the cues in *[brackets]*.

---

## Script

**[OPEN: the lesson 7.2 page, scrolled to the top]**

> This week, you open a brand new book: *Anne of Green Gables*. And your lessons look a little different. Here's a quick tour, so nothing surprises you.

**[The name card]**

> The first time you open a lesson, it asks what to call you. Type your name and press Begin. You only do this once. After that, every lesson greets you by name.

**[A play button at the top of a tab. Click it; then open "Read along" underneath]**

> See these play buttons? Each one is a short message from a guide. And here's the new part: your guides are people from the book. One day it might be Matthew Cuthbert. Another day, Marilla, or Diana, or Anne herself. The button tells you who's talking. If you'd rather read than listen, tap **Read along** and the words open up underneath.
>
> One thing to know about your guides: they only know what has happened in the story so far. They won't spoil anything. Some of them might even be surprised by what happens next, just like you.

**[The four tabs across the top]**

> Every lesson still has the same four parts: Warm-Up, Word Study, Reading, and Assignment. Work through them in order.

**[Warm-Up: the two DOL sentence cards]**

> In the Warm-Up, you now get to choose which sentence to fix. Pick A or B.

**[Word Study: type a wrong answer in a fill-in, press Check three times]**

> In Word Study, if you get stuck on a blank, don't worry. Each try gives you a bigger hint, and after three tries the answer appears so you can keep going.

**[Scroll to "Say It Three Ways"]**

> At the end of Word Study, you'll find a sentence from the book to rewrite three different ways. There's no wrong answer there.

**[Reading tab: the chapter picture, then the By Heart poem card]**

> On the Reading tab, there's a picture for the chapter. On some days there's also a poem to learn by heart. You'll keep the same poem for the whole book, and every week a few more words fade away until you can say it on your own.

**[Assignment tab: the two roads, then type into an answer box so the coaching line appears]**

> On the Assignment tab, you choose your road. Explorer, or Scholar, which asks a little more. As you type, a short note under your answer tells you if something is missing, like evidence from the book. Answer all three with evidence and you earn the Scholar's seal.

**[Writer's Workshop: the Publish button, then the illuminated page]**

> And at the bottom, your own story is waiting. When you publish, your writing turns into a page that looks like it came from an old book.

**[Back to the top of the page]**

> That's it. Your guides are waiting for you in Avonlea. Let's go meet them.

## Notes before recording

- **Check two lines against your cast decision:**
  - "This week… your guides are people from the book" is true only if week 7's clips are re-recorded in character (see the cast list).
  - If characters start in week 8, change that line to "Starting next week, your guides will be people from the book." Nothing else in the script needs to change.
- **"Anne herself"** only holds if Anne keeps at least one lesson in the cast list. In the current draft she guides 7.4, 13.4 and 14.4.
- **Kept out on purpose:** the year map and reading shelf. They're folded away by default, and one more feature would push the video past 3 minutes. They're easy to find on their own.
- **House rules followed:** no time of day, no word parts said on their own, and nothing a student needs is only in the audio. The video repeats what the pages already show.
