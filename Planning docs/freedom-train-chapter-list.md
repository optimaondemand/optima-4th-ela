## Gap closed - Ch. 8 and Ch. 11 paired into existing lessons

Ch. 8 "Riding on the Railroad" and Ch. 11 "Ship of Zion" originally had no
lesson; their absence is what pushed every downstream chapter number out of
alignment. Sept 10 2026: rather than adding lesson days (which would have
changed Canvas URLs and broken the 4-day week), the two chapters were paired
into the existing lessons that sat next to them.

| Lesson file | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `lesson-21-4-ft-ch8.html` | Ch. 9 only | **Ch. 8 & 9** - Riding on the Railroad; In a Strange Land |
| `lesson-22-2-ft-ch10.html` | Ch. 12 only | **Ch. 11 & 12** - Ship of Zion; The Old Folks |

Filenames were deliberately NOT renamed - they are the published Canvas iframe URLs.

Where Sterling's actual text is required the page carries a visible gold-dashed
**TO FILL** badge. Grep for `TO FILL` - four per file, eight in all:

- the chapter orientation paragraph (2-3 sentences of what to watch for)
- the halfway Pause & Think prompt (one specific event)
- the after-chapter Pause & Think prompt (two specific events)

Everything around those markers uses only the week's scope & sequence reading
skill (W21 "Track chronology in informational text", W22 "Explain cause and
effect in history") and facts already established by the adjacent lessons.
No plot detail was invented and no quotation was added.

## Note on "The Combahee River Raid"

Not a chapter title. The raid is real history but sits inside Ch. 16,
"Department of the South". A lesson formerly labelled "Ch. 15: The Combahee
River Raid" was rebuilt as Ch. 15 "The Most of a Man", with its Combahee
material folded into the Ch. 16 lesson.
