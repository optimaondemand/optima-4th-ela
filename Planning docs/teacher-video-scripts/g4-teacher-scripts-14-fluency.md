# Week 14 fluency teacher video script

Week 14 Fluency · your own published story. About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-14-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's fluency passage is different. You aren't reading Anne's words this time. You're reading your own story, the one you just published. That makes you the author and the reader at the same time, which is pretty special. You know better than anyone how each part should sound, so let your voice show it. If your character is nervous, let us hear that. If there's someone talking, give them a voice that fits. Slow down at the most important moment, the same moment you worked so hard on in Scene 3. And if you stumble over a sentence while you practice, that's useful information. It might be a sentence worth fixing. Be proud when you record this one.
