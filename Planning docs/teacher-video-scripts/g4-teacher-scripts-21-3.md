# Lesson 21.3 teacher video scripts

Day 3 · Ch. 7, Following the Star. Freedom Train. Guide this lesson: Marcus (college history student, former walking-tour guide on the Eastern Shore). Each script plays right after Marcus's clip in the same spot, so it adds to what he says instead of repeating it. About 40 to 60 seconds each. A gold 🎬 On screen line is a suggestion for that moment; it is never read aloud.

## Welcome · g4ela-21-3-welcome

That's a great question, and you already have a clue. Think back to Chapter 3, when you kept a tally of Harriet's teachers. Daddy Ben was on that list. He taught her things about the woods long before she needed them. Today she needs them. When Harriet wakes up in the middle of the day, watch how she calms herself down first, before she figures anything else out. She handles it in a smart order.


## Freedom Watch · g4ela-21-3-guide2

[ON SCREEN: Stop 1]

I like that Marcus turned it into a counting game. Here's a tip for keeping score. Some of Harriet's helpers aren't on the road with her at all. Listen to the directions she repeats to herself: north by east, fifteen miles. Whose words are those? That person is still helping her, even from far away. Then answer the Think question in your journal. Write about someone who helped you, and then about a time you were the helper.


## Before you read · g4ela-21-3-read

[ON SCREEN: Chapter picture]

I'd love to walk along the Choptank with Marcus someday. For Harriet, though, this walk isn't peaceful. Partway through, she hides from men who are hunting for runaways, and she stays still for a whole hour. If your heart beats faster reading it, that's okay. Keep your clock and map notes going, and add the miles. Thirty miles is about how far a car goes in half an hour. Harriet walks most of it in one night.
