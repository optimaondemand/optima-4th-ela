# Lesson 21.4 teacher video scripts

Day 4 · Ch. 8 & 9, Riding on the Railroad and In a Strange Land. Freedom Train. Guide this lesson: Marcus (college history student, former walking-tour guide on the Eastern Shore). Each script plays right after Marcus's clip in the same spot, so it adds to what he says instead of repeating it. About 40 to 60 seconds each. A gold 🎬 On screen line is a suggestion for that moment; it is never read aloud.

## Welcome · g4ela-21-4-welcome

Marcus has been circling that question for his whole paper, and today we get to think about it. Before you read, remember Harriet's dream in Chapter 5. She dreamed of a line, with people on the other side reaching out their arms to her, but she always fell before she got there. Keep that dream in mind. When Harriet comes to a real line, put the real moment right next to the dream. What matches? What's different?


## Freedom Watch · g4ela-21-4-guide2

[ON SCREEN: Stop 1]

That's a tender thing to think about every time you go home. Back in Chapter 5, Harriet had to pay Master one dollar every week, just for the chance to work. In Chapter 9, someone pays her two silver dollars, and they're all hers. That's huge. But notice what those dollars can't buy her. In your journal, answer the Think question. Write about something you wanted, got, and then found out wasn't the whole answer.


## Before you read · g4ela-21-4-read

[ON SCREEN: Chapter picture]

A room that isn't where you'd expect it. I won't say one more word about that. Here's something else to look for. At the end of Chapter 8, Sterling uses Harriet's own words, something she told a friend years later. Those aren't Sterling's words. Harriet really said them. Read them slowly, maybe twice. Also, Chapter 8 has a scary night on the road, when Harriet is so tired she can barely stay awake. Stay with her. Morning comes.
