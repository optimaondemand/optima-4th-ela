# Week 15 fluency teacher video script

Week 15 Fluency · how Denmark answered the Nazis (background reading). About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-15-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's fluency passage isn't from a novel. It's real history, four sentences about Denmark during the war, and it's the background for the book we start next week. Real history asks for a careful voice. Not sad, and not dramatic, just steady, the way you'd tell someone something important you want them to believe. Slow down on the date so your listener can hold onto it. Then listen to how the passage turns. It starts with a country giving up in a few hours, and then the word But changes everything. Let your voice lift a little on extraordinary, because that's the word the whole passage leans on. Read it three times, and record the one that sounds most like you mean it.
