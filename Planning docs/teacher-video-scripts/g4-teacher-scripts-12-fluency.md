# Week 12 fluency teacher video script

Week 12 Fluency · Anne of Green Gables, Ch. 28, after the Lily Maid. About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-12-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's passage is Anne looking back at her own mistakes, and it's one of the most grown-up things she's said so far. She isn't crying or making excuses. She's being honest, and she's even a little funny about it. Read it like someone telling a story about themselves that they can finally smile at. There's a long first sentence, so take a breath before you start and use the comma to rest. Then each mistake gets its own short sentence. Say the name of each mistake clearly, like the Haunted Wood and dyeing her hair, because those are moments you've read about. Let your voice slow down a bit at the end, so your listener can tell she's thought about this.
