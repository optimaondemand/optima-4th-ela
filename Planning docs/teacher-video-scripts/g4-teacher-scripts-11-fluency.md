# Week 11 fluency teacher video script

Week 11 Fluency · Anne of Green Gables, Ch. 21, after the liniment cake. About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-11-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's passage has two voices in it, so you get to be an actor. First there's Anne, right after the liniment cake, when she's tired and embarrassed but still hopeful. Her line is a question, so let your voice rise a little at the end, and say "a new day with no mistakes in it yet" slowly, like you're imagining it. Then comes Marilla. She isn't being mean. She's teasing Anne in her dry, matter-of-fact way, and there's a little fondness underneath. Try making your voice a bit lower and flatter for Marilla, so a listener can tell the two people apart without hearing their names. Practice the switch between them a few times before you record.
