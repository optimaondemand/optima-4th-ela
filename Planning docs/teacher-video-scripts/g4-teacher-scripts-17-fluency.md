# Week 17 fluency teacher video script

Week 17 Fluency · Number the Stars, Ch. 5 (Annemarie opens her hand). About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-17-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's fluency passage is only two sentences long, but it's one of the most important moments in the book. The soldiers have just left, and Annemarie finally opens her hand. Read the first sentence a little slower than you talk, with a soft, tired voice, like someone who has been holding their breath. Pause after looked down, just for a moment, as if you're looking too. Then read the last part softly and clearly, and let it land. Don't rush to the end. Read it three times, and record the one where a listener can feel the hush.
