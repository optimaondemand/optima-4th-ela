# Week 16 fluency teacher video script

Week 16 Fluency · Number the Stars, Ch. 1 (Annemarie answers the soldier). About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-16-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's fluency passage comes from the first chapter of Number the Stars. It's the moment Annemarie answers the soldier. Read her words politely, the way you'd answer a grown-up you don't know. Then look for the dash. That's where her voice stops in the middle of a thought, so let your voice stop there too, as if you caught yourself. The next part is what she's thinking, not saying. Read it a little quieter and quicker, like you're talking to yourself. Read the passage three times, and record the one where you can hear the difference between what she says and what she thinks.
