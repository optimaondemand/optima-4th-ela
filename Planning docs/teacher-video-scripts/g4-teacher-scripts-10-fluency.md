# Week 10 fluency teacher video script

Week 10 Fluency · Anne of Green Gables, Ch. 18, Anne saves Minnie May. About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-10-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's fluency passage comes from the night Anne saved Minnie May. Everyone else in that kitchen was frightened, and Anne was the one who knew what to do, because she'd taken care of three sets of twins before she came to Green Gables. So when you read her words out loud, don't make her sound scared. She sounds calm and quick, like someone giving directions. Notice the word "First." Anne is making a plan, step by step, and your voice can show that. Pause a little at each period, so every instruction lands. And on your recording, read at the speed you'd want someone to talk to you in an emergency: steady, clear, and sure.
