# Week 13 fluency teacher video script

Week 13 Fluency · Anne of Green Gables, Ch. 34, the night before Queen's. About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-13-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's passage is Anne talking to Marilla the night before she leaves for Queen's. Marilla is worried that the little girl she loves is gone, and Anne is gently telling her she isn't. So read it softly, the way you'd talk to someone you're trying to comfort. Watch for the dashes. Each one is a small pause, almost like Anne is touching her heart when she says, "back here." The words "pruned down and branched out" compare Anne to a tree that's been trimmed so it can grow, so say them like you're proud of it. And slow down on the last part, "your little Anne," because that's the line Marilla needs to hear.
