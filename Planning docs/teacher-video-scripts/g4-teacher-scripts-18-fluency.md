# Week 18 fluency teacher video script

Week 18 Fluency · Number the Stars, Ch. 9 (Uncle Henrik on bravery). About 30 to 60 seconds.

## Lesson video · g4ela-18-fluency

Hi, friends. This week's passage is Uncle Henrik talking with Annemarie in the barn, and it holds one of the most famous lines in the book. Read his first sentence slowly, with a tiny pause after brave, as if he is choosing every word. Then let your voice soften for the second part, where he says your mama and I. He is not giving a speech. He's talking to someone he loves. Read it three times, and record the one that sounds the kindest.
