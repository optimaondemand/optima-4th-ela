Grammar: Adjectives
3 points
Identification
1Which sentence uses adjectives correctly?
  • AHarriet was a brave, determined woman who refused to give up.
  • BHarriet was a brave determined woman who refuse to give up.
  • CHarriet was brave determined and refusing to give up.
When two adjectives both describe the same noun, they are usually separated by a comma. Check that the verb form is also correct!
Fix It
