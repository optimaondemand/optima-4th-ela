A postcard from Anne

The best drive of my whole life

Matthew Cuthbert drove me eight miles from Bright River station today. The people here gave the prettiest places such plain names, so I gave them better ones.

Will you build our drive, stop by stop, so I never forget a single bit of it?

Anne
Scope for imagination means room to imagine. Anne waited outside the station because it had more of it.

How-to video: Optima On-Demand and Canvas

These pictures come from other maps. Your map will be The Countryside, but the buttons are the same.

🖥️ First time only: connect Canvas (on your computer)

  1. 1Log in

    Go to xr.optimaed.com on your computer. Log in with the same login you use in VR.

  2. 2Connect to Canvas

    Click Connect to Canvas. Log in with your usual Canvas username and password, then click Authorize. You will see Canvas connection completed.

  3. 3Find our class

    Click Canvas on the left and pick our class. Our assignments show up. You only do this part one time.

📄 Only if the assignment has a file

  1. 4Get the file ready

    Open the assignment and click Open in Canvas to download the file. Then click My Files and Upload File.

🥽 In the headset

  1. 5Explore tab

    Put on your headset. Click the Explore tab and type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 6Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo.

  3. 7Read the assignment

    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu. Click the Canvas tab and pick our assignment to read the directions. Tip: point at a mint side grip and squeeze to pull the tablet to your hand.

  4. 8Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type the exact name in the search box, or scroll with your thumbstick. Click a model to bring it out.

  5. 9Move and resize

    Grab a model with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  6. 10Need a file?

    Click Library, then Files. Pick your file to place it in the world, next to your model.

  7. 11Record a video

    Click Library, then Camera. Move the tablet until your whole scene fits. Slide Height or the zoom bar if you need to. Switch to Video and start recording. Say your 2 sentences, then stop at 30 seconds or less.

  8. 12Pick your video

    Click the Canvas tab and pick our assignment. Click Choose from My Files, pick your newest video, then click Use this file.

  9. 13Submit to Canvas

    Click Submit to Canvas and look for the green Submitted tag. To leave VR, press the Meta button on your right controller, then click Quit.

🔁 Didn’t work in VR? Two other ways

  1. 14Use the website

    Go to xr.optimaed.com and click Canvas. Pick our assignment, click Choose from My Files, pick your video and submit it.

Your videos save to My Files at xr.optimaed.com on their own. You can also download your video there and upload it to the Canvas assignment yourself.
