Grammar: Comparative Adverbs
3 points
Identification
1Which sentence uses comparative adverbs correctly?
  • AHarriet moved more quietly in winter than in summer, and planned more carefully than before.
  • BHarriet moved more quieter in winter, and planned most carefully than before.
  • CHarriet moved quietlier in winter and planned carefullier than before.
To compare adverbs, use more ___ (not -er) for most adverbs ending in -ly. Never use both more and -er together — that is a double comparative error.
