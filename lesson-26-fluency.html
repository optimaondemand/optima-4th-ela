📖 This Week’s Passage
From Chapter 2 of Prince Caspian — the discovery of Cair Paravel
“It is my sword Rhindon,” he said. “With it I killed the Wolf.” There was a new tone in his voice, and the others all felt that he was really Peter the High King again. Then, after a little pause, everyone remembered that they must save the battery. They climbed the stair again and made up a good fire and lay down close together for warmth.

This passage is from Chapter 2 of Prince Caspian by C. S. Lewis. Peter’s voice shifts from ordinary boy to High King in a single sentence. Let your reading capture that transformation — quiet recognition, then royal authority, then the practical calm of settling in for the night. — Ch. 2, Week 26

2
🎯 The 4 Steps
Work through these in order.
1️⃣

Read for accuracy. Read the passage out loud. Say every word correctly. Slow down where you need to.

1️⃣

Read for smoothness. Read it again. This time, try not to pause or stumble. Let the sentences flow.

2️⃣

Read with expression. Read it a third time. Let your voice show the feeling — the awe of rediscovery, the shift to kingly authority.

3️⃣

Record and submit. Use Canvas’s recording tool to record your final reading. Upload it to this assignment.

3
🤔 Self-Check
Before you submit your recording.
Ask yourself:

Did I say each word correctly?

Did my reading sound smooth?

Did my voice show the feeling?

🎙️

If you can answer “yes” to all three, you’re ready to submit. If not, read it once more.
